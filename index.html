HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. Zero dashboards.

01

Forward a video

Send Precense a reel, a TikTok, a short — anything you posted or want to study. Like DMing a friend who happens to know your entire archive.

02

It finds the pattern

Precense compares the video to your history, the current wave on the platform, and the hooks that keep viewers past second three.

03

You get the next move

A short reply back in chat: what worked, why, and three specific things to make tomorrow — tuned to your voice.

WHAT IT DOES

A strategist that actually watches the videos.

Signature

Learns your voice

Every forward teaches Precense what makes a post feel like you — cadence, structure, the beats you always hit.

Patterns

Reads the wave

Tracks the formats spiking right now on your platform and maps them onto your niche — so trends actually convert.

Hooks

Writes hooks that hold

Retention-first hook ideas graded against your own best openers, not a generic viral template file.

Cadence

Tells you what's next

A tight shot list for tomorrow's post — subject, opener, pacing, CTA. Send. Shoot. Repeat.
