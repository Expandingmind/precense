WHAT IT IS TODAY

A group chat that knows the work.

Slack is for offices. iMessage is for friends. Precense is for the little studios and one-person UGC operations who need to talk about videos all day.

1:1 chats
Real-time DM anyone in your network. Send videos, images, voice-note-length riffs.
Group chats
Spin up a team room in one click. Add creators, editors, coordinators — anyone you're shipping with.
Announcements
A global feed only admins and team leads can post to. Every new member joins automatically.
Friend requests
Search by name, handle, or email. Send a request. Chat as soon as they accept.
WHAT'S IN THE APP

Small, fast, opinionated.

01

Video-first attachments

Drop a video or image straight into a chat — plays inline, no download. Made for reviewing UGC drafts in the flow.

02

Real-time everything

Typing indicators, presence dots, message delivery — all live over web sockets. No stale threads.

03

Profiles that mean something

Avatar, display name, bio. Everyone shows up as a real person, not an email address in a dropdown.

04

Google + Apple sign-in

No passwords. Two taps to an account. Your existing identity works.

05

Admin controls

Promote trusted people to team lead. Handle reports. Keep the space clean without a mod team.

06

Block + report

One-tap block on anyone. Flag messages for admin review. Trust and safety from day one.
